    Questions

    Why does ILLU need SMS access?

    Reading bank alerts is how payments reach your ledger without typing. Messages are read on your phone. OTPs and offers are ignored.

    Which banks does it work with?

    ILLU reads debit, credit, UPI, card and bank-transfer alerts from Indian banks. If one is missed, paste the message into ILLU.

    Can my family see my accounts?

    No. Account names and balances are never shared. You choose for each person whether they see totals, category totals or every entry.

    What happens after the 30 days?

    Pro features lock by themselves and nothing is charged. Everything you made stays, but nothing new can be added to it without Pro. If you share with more than one person, you pick one to keep sharing totals with. You keep receiving updates from everyone.

    Does my family need to pay?

    No. One person buys Me + partner or Whole family, and Pro reaches the other phones through the family update. No codes.

    Can I upgrade later?

    Yes. Move from Just me to a bigger plan any time by paying the difference.

    What happens if I change phones?

    Make an encrypted backup with a password you choose, then restore it on the new phone. ILLU can’t recover a forgotten password. Pro comes back through your Google Play account.

    Can I get a refund?

    Payments go through Google Play, so refunds are handled by Google Play under its refund rules.

    Is there an iPhone version?

    Not at the moment. ILLU is for Android.
